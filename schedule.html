Schedule

FacultyHack@Gateways 2026 runs as six virtual training sessions in August, followed by the in-person Gateways 2026 Conference in Washington, D.C. in September. All virtual session times are Eastern Time (ET).

Virtual Training Sessions

Six sessions, Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, August 3–14, 6:00–8:00 PM ET. Every session below joins on the same Zoom link — add the recurring event to your calendar (.ics) so you don't have to look it up each time. Click a date to collapse it. See "Standard Daily Flow" below for how each session's two hours are broken down.
